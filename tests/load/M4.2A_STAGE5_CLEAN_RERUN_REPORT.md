## 17. Interpretation

- The 100-VU knee is **REPRODUCED on a clean host**: without any
  concurrent `dotnet test`/build/k6 workload, the read workload still
  produces sustained 503s, scattered 500s, and 65 full-timeout requests,
  breaching the unweakened correctness gates while concurrency mechanics
  (`vus_max`, iterations, overlap) stay green and isolation holds.
- The verdict does not flip on a clean host, so the Stage-5 STOP stands
  and Stage 4 (50 VUs) remains the highest completed stage.

  runs by every error-magnitude measure, so the s5-A/s5-B magnitudes
  (especially s5-A's 14.6%) should be treated as plausibly
  contamination-amplified upper bounds, not as stable point estimates of
  100-VU behavior. Quantitative error-rate claims at 100 VU require a
  telemetry-instrumented environment, not more local reps.

  stably (`vus_max` 100, 300/300 iters, unique request IDs, backend-shaped
