# Product Projection Authority Matrix

R-02 certification: PROJECTION GOVERNANCE PASSED

Governance rule: Product projection Mongo mutations must be either canonical projection infrastructure or explicitly classified infrastructure-only repair, rebuild, reindex, replay, cache recovery, development seed, or auto-healing. Business controllers and business services must not directly mutate Product projection Mongo collections.

Canonical storefront authority: `ProductSqlAuthorityProjectionWriter` recomposes `ProductDocument` from SQL through `ProductSearchSqlAuthorityOverlay`, then persists through `StorefrontProductProjectionMutationBoundary` and `ProductReadRepository`.

## Governance Decisions

No business Product update may write Mongo directly. `ProductsController`, `ProductVariantsController`, `ProductMediaController`, Product Authoring, storefront services, cart services, and marketplace business services must use SQL/Product Authoring/outbox flows and let projection infrastructure update read models.

Legacy live Product projection writes: none remain unclassified. The only legacy code found is the disabled `#if false` branch in `AffiliateProductPolicyProjectionConsumer`; it is not compiled and is documented as inert legacy evidence that must not be re-enabled.

Infrastructure-only operations are explicitly limited to repair, rebuild, reindex, cache/search recovery, diagnostics, and development seed. They must not be used as business mutation entry points.

Architecture guards: `PointOfSale.Tests/Architecture/ProductProjectionGovernanceTests.cs` fails new direct Product projection Mongo mutation paths unless they are added to this matrix and classified as canonical projection or infrastructure-only.

No Product projection mutation remains unclassified.
