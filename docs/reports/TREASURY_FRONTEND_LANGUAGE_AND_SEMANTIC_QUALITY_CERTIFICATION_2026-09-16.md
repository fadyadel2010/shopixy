## 4. Discovery and Root Cause

The localization gate failed on entry for two reasons. First, five structural English peers were missing for pre-existing Arabic-only product keys (products.costPriceHint, products.posPriceHint, products.priceRequired, products.samePosPrice, products.skuPricing). Second, the expanded audit scope exposed eleven missing marketplace runtime keys (servicesMarketplace chat/timeline/completionEvidence/discover/profile/serviceDetails) plus ten further structural gaps (nav.allOrders, nav.blockedPhones, nav.cartRecovery, nav.manualPayments, nav.pixelTracking, nav.smartOffers, nav.whatsappSettings, products.simpleIdentity, products.simpleIdentityDescription, storefront.ia.design) and three report keys used by Profit and Loss (treasury.totalCogs, treasury.totalExpenses, treasury.totalRevenue). Separately, the UI pass found physical text-alignment utilities across Treasury tables, non-localized PDF/Excel export strings, UTC date truncation that can shift report days, fixed transfer quick-links pointing at retired /reports routes, a missing transfer-dialog focus routine, and stale Arabic browser-harness markers that no longer matched certified terminology.

## 6. Treasury Terminology (English and Arabic)

A shared aligned Treasury terminology set now covers dashboard, reports, ledger, clearing, transfers, statuses, periods, and actions in both languages. Added keys include treasury.periodFilters (Report period / فترة التقرير), treasury.currentClassification (Current / متداول), treasury.section via common.section (Section / القسم), treasury.accountsLateCount (Late accounts: {{count}} / عدد الحسابات المتأخرة: {{count}}), treasury.oldestDate (Oldest Date / أقدم تاريخ), treasury.ageDays (Age (Days) / العمر بالأيام), treasury.clearingAccount (Settlement account / حساب تسوية), and treasury.accountTypes for Asset, Liability, Equity, Revenue, CostOfGoodsSold, and Expense. Arabic cash wording was normalized to الخزنة النقدية and الحساب البنكي, and clearing wording was normalized to التسويات المعلقة and حسابات التسوية for consistency across Hub, Dashboard, reports, and perimeters.

## 7. Clearing Semantics

Clearing is consistently presented as money in transit that is never spendable cash and never transferable from this UI. Arabic inTransitExplanation, pendingClearing, pendingClearingTotal, clearingSeparate, clearingEvidenceBoundary, and settlementRequiresEvidence were rewritten so settlement is possible only after authenticated provider or carrier evidence inside the payment lifecycle, with no manual transfer or settlement path from Clearing Aging. Hub and Viewer role chips use treasury.clearingAccount (Settlement account / حساب تسوية) as the role label with treasury.clearingRole as the explanation, replacing the navigation-oriented clearingNav label in role context while keeping clearingNav for navigation. Clearing Aging keeps its disabled provider-workflow control and evidence boundary copy in both languages.

## 8. Available-Cash Semantics

Available cash means mapped Cashbox plus mapped Bank only, stated identically in Hub, Dashboard, Cash Flow perimeter, and transfer copy. English transferableOnly, transferableSourceRequired, and transferableDestinationRequired now say mapped Cashbox or Bank; Arabic equivalents name الخزنة النقدية and الحساب البنكي المعتمد explicitly. Dashboard cashPositionExplanation and clearingSeparate, Hub availableNowExplanation, and the Cash Flow perimeter all state that Clearing and restricted card assets are excluded from liquidity. Totals are still computed from catalog currentBalance by exact accountType; no wording change alters any total, selector population, or backend validation.

## 9. Supplier-Card Boundary Language

The supplier-card page keeps its restricted-asset boundary and now uses treasury.supplierCardRestricted in both languages: English Restricted card funding for supplier purchases and payments only, with explicit exclusions from available cash, customer-card clearing, and transfer destinations; Arabic هذا الرصيد أصل مالي مقيد لمشتريات ومدفوعات الموردين فقط with the same three exclusions. The page table uses scope=col headers, logical text-start alignment, locale-aware statement dates, bdi isolation for identifiers, and stackable summary grids. Supplier-card rows remain outside the Treasury catalog, Hub totals, Dashboard cash position, and transfer selectors, and existing Owner/Admin/Manager access semantics are unchanged.

## 10. Report Perimeter Language

Cash Flow, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, and General Ledger each render a role=note perimeter in both languages. Cash Flow limits cash to mapped Cashbox and Bank and excludes Clearing and restricted card assets. Balance Sheet states that Assets are broader and may include settlement accounts, supplier-card funding, control accounts, and other non-cash rows, with only mapped Cashbox and Bank as available cash. Trial Balance states it is the complete ledger rather than the available-cash catalog and that inclusion grants no transfer or settlement authority. General Ledger states that all ledger accounts and complete history are shown, including settlement accounts, supplier-card assets, account 1020, controls, and reserves, with the same no-authority rule. Browser certification asserts role=note presence on cash-flow, balance-sheet, trial-balance, and general-ledger in all four language/viewport combinations.

## 11. Source-Type and Account-Type Labels

General Ledger and Account Viewer no longer render raw backend sourceType codes as user-facing English. Both resolve treasury.sourceTypes.<Code> with a localized Other source fallback (English Other source / Arabic مصدر آخر), covering SalesInvoice, SalesInvoice_COGS, PurchaseInvoice, SupplierPayment, opening balances, adjustments, returns and their COGS reversals, online order payment/clearance/COD/wallet/refund codes, storefront and CommerceHub COGS postings and reversals, gateway and COD settlements, CommerceHub financial transactions, affiliate commission paid/earned, SupplierCardFunding, TreasuryInitialCapitalization, Treasury deposit/withdraw/transfer/expense/cash-in/cash-out, ClearingSettlement, BankDeposit, and Reversal. Unknown future backend codes fall back to Other source without inventing authority.

## 12. Export Localization (Excel and PDF)

## 13. Date, Number, and Currency Localization

A shared helper pair was added to PointOfSale.Web/src/utils/dateFormat.js: parseDateInput parses YYYY-MM-DD as a local calendar date and rejects blank or impossible dates, and formatDateInputValue formats a Date back to YYYY-MM-DD without UTC conversion. All six Treasury report surfaces (Trial Balance, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, General Ledger, Clearing Aging) plus Account Viewer and the supplier-card page use these helpers for native date inputs, API query parameters via services/financialReportsApi.js, and export filenames. This removes the prior new Date(value) UTC parse and toISOString truncation defects that could shift a report day or inject an invalid Date into state. Currency and number rendering remain locale-aware through SettingsContext formatCurrency and Intl-backed helpers, including Arabic ar-EG bidi sign handling covered by existing tests.
