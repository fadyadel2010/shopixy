# Product Mutation Authority Matrix

R-01 certification: LEGACY PRODUCT MUTATION GOVERNANCE PASSED

Governance rule: business Product mutations must execute through `ProductAuthoringOrchestrator`. A bypass is allowed only when it is infrastructure-only and does not modify authoritative Product semantics.

| Mutation | Classification | Current entry point | Current authority | Canonical authority | Business justification | Governance decision and migration strategy | Source evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product create | Canonical | `ProductsController.CreateProduct` | Product Authoring | Product Authoring | Creates authoritative Product aggregate, units, variants, outbox, idempotency, audit. | Keep. | `PointOfSale.API/Controllers/ProductsController.cs` uses `_productAuthoringOrchestrator.AuthorAsync`; `PointOfSale.API/Services/ProductAuthoring/ProductAuthoringOrchestrator.cs` owns `ExecuteCreateAsync`. |
| Product update | Canonical | `ProductsController.UpdateProduct` | Product Authoring | Product Authoring | Updates authoritative Product business state with Expected-Version concurrency. | Keep. | `ProductsController.UpdateProduct`; `ProductAuthoringOrchestrator.ExecuteUpdateAsync`. |
| Product remove/archive | Canonical | `ProductsController.DeleteProduct`, `ProductsController.DeactivateProduct` | Product Authoring | Product Authoring | Lifecycle mutation of authoritative Product and live variants. | Keep. | `ProductsController.DeleteProduct`; `ProductsController.DeactivateProduct`; `ProductAuthoringOrchestrator.ExecuteArchiveOrRemoveAsync`. |
| Product units add/update/delete | Canonical | `ProductsController.AddProductUnit`, `ProductsController.UpdateProductUnit`, `ProductsController.DeleteProductUnit` | Product Authoring | Product Authoring | ProductUnit membership is authored Product state. | Keep. | Controller methods call `_productAuthoringCommandMapper.FromProductUnitsUpdate` and `_productAuthoringOrchestrator.AuthorAsync`. |

| Variant stock update | Infrastructure-only | `ProductVariantsController.UpdateVariantStock` | Inventory stock authority | Inventory stock authority | Mutates `ProductVariantStock` quantity and stock events, not authored Product business fields. | Keep as inventory operation. | Uses `ProductVariantStockMutationSupport.LockStockForMutationAsync` and `PublishCommittedStockEventsAsync`. |

No Product mutation remains unknown. Legacy business mutation decisions are explicit: variant CRUD/default and multi-unit migration are deprecated; Product CRUD/lifecycle/unit writes route through Product Authoring; stock, media projection touch, search, Mongo, and seed tooling are infrastructure-only with source-code justification.
