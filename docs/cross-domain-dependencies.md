## Required Dependency Examples

Inventory -> Product:

- Present and intentional. Stock state belongs to Inventory and references Product through Variant identity/lineage. Evidence: `ProductVariantStock.cs:28`, `ProductVariant.cs:38`, `ProductVariantStockRepository.cs:98`, `:122`, `ProductVariantInventoryLineageAuthority.cs:15-56`.

Purchasing -> Pricing:

- Present and failing. Purchasing/Purchase Returns directly mutate canonical cost/purchase-price fields. Evidence: `PurchasesController.cs:219`, `:1133`, `:1461`; `PurchaseReturnsController.cs:540`, `:550`; `ProductAuthoringOrchestrator.cs:1350`.

Checkout -> Inventory:

- Present and intentional. Checkout calls stock repository/reservation APIs and does not claim stock ownership. Evidence: `CheckoutService.cs:5261`, `:5850`; `ProductVariantStockRepository.cs:14`, `:165`, `:312`, `:605-606`.

CommerceHub -> Catalog:

- Present and intentional. CommerceHub reads Product/Variant authority and owns listing/channel state. Evidence: `CommerceHubCommandHandlers.cs:319`, `:566`; `CommerceHubSearchSqlAuthorityOverlay.cs:78-100`, `:139-159`; `CommerceHubListing.cs:14-21`.

Affiliate -> Catalog:

- Present and intentional. Affiliate validates Product eligibility and creates affiliate policy/link state; it does not own Product catalog fields. Evidence: `AffiliatePromotionLinksController.cs:74-88`, `:301-352`; `AffiliateService.cs:433`, `:470`, `:500`.

## Final Dependency Assessment

No Product-affecting dependency remains unidentified. Most dependencies are legitimate read/projection or inventory interactions. Certification fails because Purchasing -> Pricing/Product cost updates are live cross-domain business mutations with split authority and no explicit ACL, and because shared EF entity access leaves enforcement dependent on convention in several Inventory-adjacent flows.
